A Clean Battle!

Unity Multiplayer Study, Complex Systems and UI animation

The main point

I started A Clean Battle with the intent of learning Unity Netcode and working with a more complex Multiplayer game. I had the idea for a Multiplayer Roguelike Deckbuilder quite a while before I wanted to learning this side of Unity and I started with a paper prototype. The main Idea for the game is to fight together to defeat harder and harder bosses, gathering upgrades for your cards on the way

Gameplay

The Gameplay has many elements. Cards cost action points, can deal damage or inflict status effects and generate threat which is used to determine who will be targeted by your enemy. The enemie's moves are shown in advance so you and your teammates can play around it. The game is balanced around teamwork and encourages communication because a lot of cards and effects play off of each other.

UI animation and dynamic scaling

Creating a 2D card game brings a lot of challenges. The underlying systems need to be represented clearly in the UI and not every device has the same display-size. One thing that was important to me with this project, was putting polish into the game as soon as possible, which resulted in these card animations using dotween.

Multiplayer and Netcode

Setting the game up to work for multiple people was the greatest challenge and what I got lost in the most. Uni and private life meant a lot of stress and sadly the game never got finished, although the basics, like joining a room together and choosing a role were already implemented.
